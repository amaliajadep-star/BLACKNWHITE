01 / THE BIGGER PICTURE

THE WORLD
ISN'T JUST
BLACK AND WHITE.

The American Civil Rights Movement challenged a society built on racial segregation and inequality. Across the 1950s and 1960s, ordinary people organised, protested and demanded change — challenging laws, institutions and attitudes that denied Black Americans equal rights.

BLACKNWHITE. explores the history behind the movement, the people who shaped it and the changes that continue to influence society today.
