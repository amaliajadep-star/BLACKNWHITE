This index brings together the external sources used to research the Civil Rights Movement and its effects on American society. The sources include government archives, historical collections, educational resources and statistical information.

A NOTE ON THE RESEARCH

DON'T JUST
TAKE OUR WORD.

BLACKNWHITE. uses historical evidence and published research to explain the movement. Whenever possible, original documents and official records are included so readers can investigate the information themselves.

LOOK BEYOND THE IMAGE.

A photograph can show what a moment looked like, but its catalogue record can provide additional information such as the date, location, photographer and historical context. Always check the original record when using a photograph as evidence.
